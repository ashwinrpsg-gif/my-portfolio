ML + NLP Application

PlayGuard AI — Play Store Safety & Trust Inspector

A Python application that analyses Google Play app information and user reviews to help identify potential trust and safety concerns.

  • Collects app metadata and up to 250 relevant English-language reviews.
  • Uses a TF-IDF and Multinomial Naive Bayes model to classify potentially spam or fake reviews.
  • Uses pretrained DistilBERT sentiment analysis to estimate review sentiment.
  • Combines keyword/rule checks and a 0–100 risk score to present a LOW, MEDIUM, or HIGH risk indication.
  • Displays findings in a Streamlit interface with Plotly visualisations.
PythonStreamlitscikit-learnDistilBERTPlotlyGoogle Play Scraper

The risk indication is a project-generated estimate, not an official Google Play safety rating.
